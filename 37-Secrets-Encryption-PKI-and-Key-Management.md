# Lesson 37 --- Secrets, Encryption, PKI and Key Management

## Distinguish

-   Encryption protects confidentiality.
-   Hashing is one-way transformation.
-   HMAC provides keyed integrity/authentication.
-   Digital signatures provide authenticity/integrity.
-   Encoding is not encryption.

## TLS

Protects data in transit.

## Key management

Use managed key/secrets systems where appropriate. Plan: - Rotation -
Access control - Audit - Recovery - Key identifiers - Crypto agility

## Workload identity

Prefer short-lived identity-based access over long-lived secrets.

## Passwords

Use a password hashing algorithm designed for passwords with salts and
appropriate cost parameters.
